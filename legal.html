General Terms of Service (B2C & B2B)

1.1 Platform Role & Intermediary Disclaimer

The HABACHA Platform operates exclusively as an international Lead Generation Intermediary. We connect private renters (B2C) and corporate enterprises (B2B) with verified independent fleet operators and asset suppliers. HABACHA does not own, manage, maintain, or operate any of the assets listed on the platform.

HABACHA does not process direct rental payments, hold security deposits, or issue insurance policies. All financial transactions and final rental agreements are executed solely on the Supplier’s official gateway or physical branch location.

1.2 Referral Codes (HB-xxxx)

Upon submitting an inquiry, Clients receive a unique tracking code (e.g., HB-xxxx). This code guarantees priority routing to the selected Supplier. The code does not constitute a legally binding reservation until confirmed directly by the Supplier.

1.3 Limitation of Liability

HABACHA shall not be held liable for any damages, personal injuries, delays, asset unavailability, mechanical failures, or financial disputes arising from the rental or lease agreement between the Client and the Supplier. The Supplier assumes full operational and legal liability for the asset.

Supplier Partnership Agreement

2.1 Supplier Obligations & Fleet Standards

By registering as a Fleet Operator ("Supplier") and submitting assets for moderation, the Supplier guarantees that all listed units are legally owned or authorized, commercially insured, and meet international safety and maintenance standards.

2.2 Commercial Intermediary Commission

HABACHA operates on a performance-based intermediary model. The Supplier agrees to remit a standard Intermediary Commission Fee of [XX]% (as defined in the final bilateral SLA) on the total base rental value of any booking successfully finalized and paid by a Client referred via a HABACHA Referral Code (HB-xxxx).

Commission invoices are generated monthly based on verified and completed bookings. HABACHA does not charge listing fees or mandatory subscription costs; fees are strictly "No Cure, No Pay."

2.3 Asset Moderation & Removal

HABACHA Admin reserves the absolute right to reject, modify, or remove any asset listing from the global directory if it violates platform quality standards, displays inaccurate pricing, or receives consistent negative feedback from enterprise clients.

Global Privacy Policy (GDPR / CCPA)

3.1 Data Collection

As a lead generator, HABACHA collects minimal necessary data, including Full Names, Corporate Emails, Business Phone Numbers, and basic operational requirements (e.g., License Types). We do not collect or store Credit Card details, Passport copies, or financial credentials.

3.2 Data Transmission to Suppliers

By clicking "Generate Referral Code", the Client explicitly consents to the transmission of their submitted inquiry data directly to the chosen Supplier to facilitate the rental request.

3.3 Right to Erasure

Under international privacy laws (GDPR/CCPA), users may request the total deletion of their platform account and inquiry history by contacting HABACHA Admin via the portal.

Cookie & Tracking Policy

HABACHA utilizes strictly necessary functional cookies and referral-tracking mechanisms to ensure that leads generated on this platform are accurately attributed to the correct Supplier via the HB-xxxx codes.

  • Session Cookies: Used to maintain Client and Supplier dashboard logins.
  • Referral Tracking: Temporary local storage used to pass parameters across the platform safely.
  • No aggressive third-party advertising trackers are deployed on the core exchange pages.
