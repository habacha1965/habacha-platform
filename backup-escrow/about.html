The HABACHA Standard

HABACHA is the premier global exchange for high-value asset allocation. We connect Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals and global enterprises with a curated network of verified suppliers. Curation & Vetting

Unlike consumer marketplaces, we do not allow direct listings. Every asset is reviewed by our Curation Team, and every client is vetted for financial capability. We operate strictly under institutional Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to protect the identity of our clients and the off-market status of our assets.
