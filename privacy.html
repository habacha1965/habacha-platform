Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect basic information necessary to facilitate your requests: Full Name, Email Address, and the specific details of your asset inquiry (e.g., category, dates, preferences).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to generate tracking codes, send transactional emails, and connect you with the relevant supplier. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

When you submit a request, your contact details and request parameters are shared with the specific supplier you are matched with to fulfill your inquiry.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are within the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. Please contact our data protection team to exercise these rights.
