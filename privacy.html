Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data HABACHA collects, why, how it is stored, and what rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses the HABACHA platform: clients, suppliers, and visitors.

HABACHA is the "data controller" for the personal data described here. That means we decide why and how your data is processed.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Controller: HABACHA
Legal form: Registered in the Netherlands
Contact for privacy matters: service4companys@gmail.com (subject line: "Privacy")
Data Protection: Because HABACHA does not yet process special categories of personal data at scale, we do not have a formal DPO. The founder acts as the point of contact for data protection questions until a DPO is formally appointed.

2. What data we collect

2.1 From clients

  • Your full name.
  • Your email address.
  • The details of your request (division, HB-Code, and any optional preferences you provide, such as "skipper required" or "number of guests").

2.2 From suppliers

  • Company name, contact email address, and website URL.
  • Your unique contract link.
  • A hashed version of your password (we never store the plain-text password).
  • Your division, and the status of your application (pending, approved, rejected, or deleted).

2.3 From all visitors

  • Technical data generated by your browser (IP address, browser type, and pages visited). This is processed by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and performance monitoring.
  • Any data you voluntarily provide via the contact form.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment card data. Payments are handled directly between clients and suppliers, outside the platform.
  • We do not collect your ID documents, driver's license, or boat license. Those are verified by the supplier directly.
  • We do not collect health data, biometric data, or any other special category of personal data.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Client request data (HB-Codes): kept for 24 months from the date of the request, to allow us to resolve any later dispute about the referral. After 24 months, the data is deleted.
  • Supplier account data: kept for as long as your account is active. After account termination, data is kept for 7 years to comply with Dutch tax law (fiscal retention period), then deleted.
  • Contact form submissions: kept for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Technical logs: kept for 30 days, then deleted by the hosting provider.

5. Who we share your data with

5.1 The matched supplier

When you submit a request, your name, email, HB-Code, and request details are sent to the supplier you are matched with. This is necessary for the supplier to contact you and complete the booking.

Some of our providers are based in the United States. When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure an adequate level of protection. You can request a copy of the SCCs by emailing us.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — to receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to have incorrect data corrected.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to have your data deleted, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to restriction — to limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, to withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email service4companys@gmail.com with the subject line "GDPR Request". We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act.

Right to complain. If you believe we have handled your data incorrectly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. You may also complain in your country of residence within the EU.

7. Security measures

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with SHA-256 before storage. We cannot recover your password.
  • Access to the database is restricted to the founder and is protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Suppliers can only see the leads assigned to their own division.
  • Payment data (card numbers, etc.) never reaches our servers — the platform does not process payments.

8. Children

HABACHA is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted data through the platform, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. If a change is material, we will notify active suppliers by email. Continued use of the platform after the change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, email service4companys@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy". We respond within 30 days.
