Fleet Operator Partnership Agreement

Tiered Commission Framework (No Cure, No Pay)

1. Operational Model

HABACHA provides fleet operators with qualified international client referrals via a transparent referral code system (HB-xxxx). Suppliers maintain full discretion over final pricing, availability, and contract terms.

2. Tiered Commission Structure

Standard Tier

20% Commission

Applies to fleet operators listing fewer than 25 active assets on the exchange.

Enterprise Volume Tier

15% Commission

Applies to enterprise operators listing 25+ assets or holding exclusive regional distribution contracts.

Commissions are billed monthly strictly on successfully completed rentals originated via HABACHA tracking codes.

3. Fleet Compliance & Insurance Requirements

Suppliers warrant that all listed assets possess valid commercial liability insurance, maritime/aviation certifications where applicable, and official registry documentation uploaded during onboarding.
