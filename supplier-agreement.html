Supplier Agreement

Last updated: October 2026

1. Onboarding & Approval

All suppliers must register with accurate company details and a unique contract link. HABACHA reserves the right to vet and approve all suppliers to maintain platform quality.

2. The 50% Founding Discount

Approved founding suppliers are eligible for a 50% discount on their first-year platform fees, subject to compliance with these terms.

3. Referral Obligations

When a client arrives at your site with an HB-code, you are obligated to recognize this as a HABACHA referral. Suppliers must log into their portal to mark leads as "Booked" to ensure accurate commission tracking.

4. Contract Links

Suppliers must provide a valid, secure booking URL. HABACHA is not responsible for broken links or technical errors on the supplier's website.
