Supplier Agreement

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Supplier Agreement is between HABACHA and any person or entity that registers as a Supplier on the HABACHA platform. It supplements the general Terms of Service. In case of conflict, this agreement prevails for Supplier-specific matters.

1. Definitions

  • "Supplier" refers to the registered company operating a fleet or asset inventory on HABACHA.
  • "Lead" refers to a Client request that HABACHA routes to the Supplier using the Supplier's contract link.
  • "HB-Code" refers to the unique tracking code attached to each Lead.
  • "Commission" refers to the fee owed by the Supplier to HABACHA for each confirmed Lead.
  • "Confirmed Lead" refers to a Lead that the Supplier has marked as Booked in the HABACHA Supplier portal, or that HABACHA has otherwise verified as concluded.

2. Onboarding and Approval

Suppliers register through the supplier registration page. Registration requires a company name, contact email, password, division, website URL, and a unique contract link. HABACHA reviews each application manually before approval. HABACHA may reject any application at its sole discretion.

3. The Contract Link

The contract link is the URL on the Supplier's website where a Client can complete a booking. When HABACHA routes a Lead to the Supplier, the HB-Code is appended to this URL. For example: https://supplier.com/book?ref=HB-8F3K9A2B7X. The Supplier is responsible for ensuring that the contract link is valid, secure, and functional.

5. Founding Supplier Discount

Suppliers who are approved during HABACHA's founding phase receive a 50% discount on the applicable Commission for the first 12 months from the date of approval. After 12 months, the standard rate applies. The discount is personal to the Supplier, cannot be transferred, and cannot be combined with any other promotional offer.

6. Reporting and Invoicing

The Supplier reports each Confirmed Lead through the Supplier portal by clicking "Mark as Booked" and entering the final transaction value. HABACHA issues a consolidated invoice at the end of each calendar month based on the Confirmed Leads reported during that month.

The invoice specifies the HB-Codes, the reported transaction values, and the applicable Commission. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. Service Level

Suppliers are expected to:

  • Acknowledge each Lead within 48 hours (either by contacting the Client or by updating the status in the portal).
  • Mark Leads as Booked within 7 days of the booking being confirmed.
  • Report transaction values accurately.

Repeated failure to acknowledge Leads or to report bookings may result in suspension or termination.

8. Insurance and Compliance

The Supplier must maintain all licenses, permits, and insurance required by the laws of the jurisdiction in which it operates. This includes, where applicable, motor insurance, marine insurance, aviation insurance, and public liability insurance. HABACHA does not provide insurance for the Supplier's assets or operations.

9. Independence of the Parties

HABACHA and the Supplier are independent parties. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship, a partnership, a joint venture, or an agency relationship. The Supplier is not authorised to make commitments on behalf of HABACHA.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public information they receive from the other party in connection with this agreement, including HB-Codes, transaction values, and Client contact details. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for a period of 2 years.

11. Indemnification

The Supplier agrees to indemnify HABACHA against any claim, damage, or cost (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:

  • The Supplier's failure to perform a booked rental or lease.
  • Any injury, loss, or damage caused by the Supplier's assets or personnel.
  • Any breach by the Supplier of applicable law, licensing requirements, or these terms.

12. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement at any time by written notice. HABACHA may terminate immediately if the Supplier breaches these terms, fails to pay an invoice, or engages in conduct that harms the platform. On termination:

  • The Supplier's portal access is revoked.
  • Any outstanding Commission remains payable.
  • Confidentiality obligations survive.

13. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Disputes will be submitted to the competent court in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.
