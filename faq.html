Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Short answers to the questions we are asked most often. If your question is not here, use the contact form.

General

What is HABACHA?

HABACHA is an intermediary platform. You submit a request for a rental or lease, and we match you with a vetted supplier in one of our eight divisions. You receive a unique tracking code (the HB-Code) and complete the deal directly with that supplier.

Is HABACHA a rental company?

No. We do not own or operate any of the assets listed on the platform. Every vehicle, vessel, aircraft, machine, or property is owned by an independent supplier. HABACHA routes your request to them.

Where is HABACHA based?

HABACHA is operated from the Netherlands. Dutch law governs our terms and privacy practices.

For Clients

Does it cost anything to submit a request?

No. Submitting a request and receiving an HB-Code is free. You only pay the supplier directly for the asset you rent or lease.

Do I need an account?

No. You provide your name and email address, and we generate your HB-Code. There is no password to remember and no profile to maintain.

What is an HB-Code?

An HB-Code is a unique 10-character code (example: HB-8F3K9A2B7X) attached to your request. It is your proof of referral and is sent to your email address. Keep it — you will need it if you contact the supplier or us about the booking.

What happens after I receive my HB-Code?

You are automatically redirected to the supplier's booking page with your HB-Code attached. The supplier then confirms the deal in their HABACHA portal. You will not hear from HABACHA again unless there is a problem with the referral.

What if the supplier does not respond?

Contact the supplier directly using your HB-Code. If the issue is not resolved within 5 business days, email us at service4companys@gmail.com with your HB-Code and we will follow up on the referral.

For Suppliers

How do I become a supplier?

Register on the supplier registration page. Provide your company name, contact email, a password, your division, your website URL, and a unique contract link. Our team reviews each application manually before approval.

How long does approval take?

We aim to respond within 24 to 48 hours. During the founding phase, review times may be longer.

What is the contract link?

It is the URL on your website where a client can complete a booking. When HABACHA refers a client to you, their HB-Code is appended to this URL. Example: if your contract link is https://yourcompany.com/book?ref=, the client will arrive at https://yourcompany.com/book?ref=HB-8F3K9A2B7X.

How do I receive leads?

Once approved, leads for your division appear in your supplier portal. Log in with your registered email and password to view them.

How do I confirm a booking?

In the supplier portal, click Mark as Booked on the relevant lead. This records the deal in HABACHA's system and triggers the commission process.

Payments & Commissions

What commission does HABACHA charge?

Commission varies by division:

  • Cars & Vans — 8%
  • Commercial Freight — 10%
  • Boats & Yachts — 15%
  • Helicopters & Aircraft — 15%
  • Stays & Real Estate — 12%
  • Construction Machinery — 12%
  • Campers & RVs — 10%
  • Event & AV Equipment — 15%

What is the Founding Supplier discount?

Suppliers who join during HABACHA's founding phase pay 50% of the standard commission for their first 12 months. After that period, the standard rate applies. The discount cannot be extended or transferred.

How am I invoiced?

HABACHA issues an invoice at the end of each calendar month based on the leads you marked as Booked during that month. The invoice specifies the HB-Code, the booking value you reported, and the applicable commission. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date.

How do I report a booking value?

When you mark a lead as Booked in the portal, you also enter the final transaction value. HABACHA uses this figure to calculate the commission. If you need to correct a value after the fact, contact us within 7 days.

Privacy & Security

What data does HABACHA store?

For clients: your name, email address, and the details of your request (division, HB-Code, optional preferences such as "skipper required"). For suppliers: company name, contact email, hashed password, division, website URL, and contract link. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is my password stored securely?

Yes. Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before storage. We never store plain-text passwords and cannot recover them — this is why the password reset flow exists.

Do you share my data with third parties?

Only with the supplier you are matched with (for clients) and with the infrastructure providers listed in our Privacy Policy (Supabase, Vercel, Resend). We do not sell data.

Technical

What if the redirect to the supplier fails?

Your HB-Code is in the URL. Copy the code from your confirmation email and contact the supplier directly, mentioning the code. If the supplier cannot resolve it, email us at service4companys@gmail.com with the code and a description of what happened.

Which browsers are supported?

HABACHA works on any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) and on mobile. It does not support Internet Explorer.
