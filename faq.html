Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the HABACHA global intermediary platform.

How does HABACHA work?
HABACHA is a global intermediary platform. You submit one request, we generate a unique tracking code (HB-code), and connect you directly with verified suppliers across our 8 divisions. Final agreements are made directly between you and the supplier.
Is HABACHA free for clients?
Yes. Submitting a request on HABACHA is completely free for clients. You only pay the supplier directly for the asset you rent or lease. HABACHA earns a commission from the supplier, not from you.
What is an HB-code?
An HB-code is a unique 10-character tracking code (e.g., HB-8F3K9A2B7X) generated for your request. It is your proof of referral and is sent to you by email. Keep it safe for your records.
How are suppliers vetted?
Every supplier goes through our approval process. We verify their company details, website, and contract link before they can receive leads. Only approved suppliers appear in our global network.
What happens after I submit a request?
You receive your HB-code by email, and are automatically redirected to the supplier's website to complete your booking. The supplier will then confirm the deal in their HABACHA portal.
Can I track my bookings?
Yes. Your HB-code is your tracking reference. Contact the supplier directly with your HB-code for status updates. A client dashboard will be launched in a future update.
How do suppliers join HABACHA?
Visit our Supplier Registration page. Fill in your company details, choose your division, and provide a unique contract link. Once approved by our team, you can start receiving high-intent leads.
What is the Founding Supplier discount?
Our founding suppliers receive a 50% discount on their first-year platform fees. This offer is limited to early applicants and can be revoked at any time. See the Supplier Agreement for full terms.
How is my data protected?
We take data protection seriously. Read our full Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details on how we handle and protect your information.
Which countries do you operate in?
HABACHA operates worldwide. Our network of suppliers spans multiple countries and we are continuously onboarding vetted partners across all 8 divisions.
