Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide a seamless experience on the HABACHA platform.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain session states (e.g., when you log into the Admin or Supplier portals). We also use analytical cookies (via third parties) to understand how users interact with our homepage and forms.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging into the Supplier or Admin portals.
