1. Nature of the Platform

HABACHA acts exclusively as an international intermediary exchange platform connecting verified fleet suppliers with private and corporate enterprise clients. HABACHA does not own, operate, charter, or directly lease any of the listed assets. All rental contracts and financial settlements occur directly between the client and the respective asset supplier.

2. Referral Tracking & Gateway Protocol

Upon submitting an inquiry on HABACHA, clients receive a unique tracking referral code (HB-xxxx). Clients are redirected to the supplier's verified direct booking gateway. HABACHA captures inquiry metadata strictly for verification and referral attribution.

3. Vehicle Pickup & Logistics Policy

Passenger vehicles and commercial fleets listed on HABACHA are under strict compliance rules. Delivery to private residential addresses is strictly prohibited. All vehicle handovers must occur at official airport terminal rental desks or licensed supplier branch offices.

HABACHA shall not be held liable for operational delays, asset defects, insurance claims, or contractual disputes arising between the client and the supplier. Suppliers are solely responsible for compliance with international maritime, aviation, and transport regulations.
