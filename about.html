About HABACHA

Last updated: October 2026

HABACHA is a global intermediary platform connecting clients with verified rental and leasing suppliers across 8 distinct divisions.

Our mission is to streamline the asset acquisition process. Whether you are a corporate enterprise seeking long-term industrial leases or an individual looking for a premium vehicle or private yacht, HABACHA provides a single request gateway to a vast network of trusted suppliers.

Our 8 Divisions

  • Cars & Vans
  • Commercial Freight
  • Boats & Yachts
  • Helicopters & Aircraft
  • Stays & Real Estate
  • Construction Machinery
  • Campers & RVs
  • Event & AV Equipment

HABACHA does not own the assets listed. We act as an intermediary, generating unique tracking referrals to connect you directly with the ultimate supplier. Final contractual agreements, pricing, and delivery are established directly between the client and the supplier.
