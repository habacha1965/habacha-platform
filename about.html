About HABACHA

Last updated: 30 October 2026

HABACHA exists because finding the right rental or leasing partner takes too long. A client who needs a yacht for a week, an excavator for a project, or a corporate van for the summer starts by calling around. Ten calls later, three quotes, no clear comparison. Meanwhile, suppliers spend more time chasing leads than serving customers.

HABACHA reduces that to a single request. You tell us what you need. We match you with a vetted supplier in one of our eight divisions. You receive a unique tracking code — the HB-Code — and continue directly with that supplier. No account required. No fee for clients.

What we are, and what we are not

We are an intermediary platform. We do not own the cars, boats, aircraft, machinery, or properties listed on this site. We do not set the prices. We do not handle the payments between you and the supplier. What we do is route your request to the right supplier and keep a record of that referral.

This is a deliberate choice. If we owned the assets, we would be a rental company. If we handled payments, we would be a financial service. HABACHA operates as the efficient middle layer between clients and the operators who actually do the work.

Our eight divisions

HABACHA operates across eight asset divisions. Each has its own supplier network, its own compliance requirements, and its own commission structure. A yacht charter is not the same as a van rental, and we do not pretend it is.

  • Cars & Vans — passenger vehicles, SUVs, executive cars, and passenger vans
  • Commercial Freight — cargo vans, heavy trucks, and trailers
  • Boats & Yachts — sailing yachts, motor yachts, catamarans, and cargo vessels
  • Helicopters & Aircraft — private jets, helicopters, and charter aircraft
  • Stays & Real Estate — corporate apartments, villas, and commercial space
  • Construction Machinery — excavators, cranes, loaders, and industrial equipment
  • Campers & RVs — motorhomes and campervans
  • Event & AV Equipment — sound, lighting, and production gear

Where we are now

HABACHA is in its founding phase. We are actively onboarding our first cohort of suppliers and are not yet operating at full capacity across every division. We mention this because we prefer to be accurate about where we stand. Founding suppliers receive a 50% commission discount for their first 12 months on the platform.

Clients pay nothing to use HABACHA. Submitting a request, receiving an HB-Code, and being redirected to a supplier is completely free.

How we make money

HABACHA earns a commission from the supplier when a referred deal closes. Clients do not pay a fee. The commission varies by division, between 8% and 15% of the transaction value, and is fully disclosed in the Supplier Agreement. Founding suppliers pay half of the standard rate for their first 12 months.

What we value

  • Accuracy over hype. We describe what we do, not what we wish we did. When something is still being built, we say so.
  • A fair split. Suppliers do the operational work. We take a commission that reflects the value of the referral, not more.
  • Transparency by default. Our terms, privacy policy, commission rates, and the limits of our service are all public documents.
